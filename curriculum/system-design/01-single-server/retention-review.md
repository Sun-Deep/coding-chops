# Previous short-form retention review

## Observed

- Approximately 3,000 Facebook views
- About 85 percent of viewers did not remain beyond five seconds

## Supported conclusion

The opening did not retain most of the reached audience. The first five seconds deserve focused testing.

## Not yet proven

- That the visual aesthetic was blameless
- That length alone caused the result
- That the platform algorithm penalized the upload
- That increasing motion frequency alone will improve qualified retention

## Next tests

Test one variable at a time where practical:

1. Immediate active diagram compared with a static title card
2. Direct question compared with documentary setup
3. Complete 30 to 60 second micro-lesson compared with a long vertical master
4. Same hook with restrained motion compared with frequent decorative motion

Success should include comprehension and meaningful engagement, not retention alone.
