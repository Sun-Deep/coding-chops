# Sources

## NIST Dictionary of Algorithms and Data Structures

Paul E. Black, ["towers of Hanoi"](https://www.nist.gov/dads/HTML/towersOfHanoi.html),
Dictionary of Algorithms and Data Structures, updated April 21, 2022.

Used for the problem rules, the three-stage recursive solution and the minimum
move count `2^n - 1`.

## MIT OpenCourseWare

[Lecture 07: Recurrences](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-1200j-mathematics-for-computer-science-spring-2024/mit6_1200j_s24_lec07.pdf),
6.1200J Mathematics for Computer Science, Spring 2024.

Used to check the recurrence and the legal-move rules. Applying that recurrence
to four plates gives fifteen moves.
