- [ ] Explain what avalanche means and what number a one character change should move, out of what.
- [ ] Say why the reel counts three survivors rather than sixty-one changes, when both are true of the same event.
- [ ] Explain why 146 bits is not the headline figure and 128.0 is.
- [ ] Say why a hex character survives about one time in sixteen.
- [ ] Explain why the right password reproduces the stored row exactly, every time.
- [ ] Say what goes wrong when two accounts pick the same password, and what a salt does about it.
