# Understanding check

The creator gate. Answer each without the notes, the script or the render.

- [ ] Say why every one of the six opens exactly 299 walls on a grid of 300 rooms, and what would be wrong with a maze that opened 300.
- [ ] Explain why that number is what lets all six share one clock and finish on the same frame.
- [ ] Describe how depth-first builds a maze, and why that leaves long corridors and few dead ends.
- [ ] Describe how Prim's builds one, and why its route is nearly direct while it has the most dead ends of the six.
- [ ] Explain what Kruskal's checks before it knocks a wall down, and what that check is protecting.
- [ ] Say what Wilson's does with a loop in its walk, and what is special about the mazes it produces.
- [ ] Explain why a binary tree maze always has an open top row and an open right column.
- [ ] Say why its route from top left to bottom right is always 36 rooms on this grid, whatever the seed.
- [ ] Explain why sidewinder shares the open top row but not the open right column.
- [ ] Say why a short route does not make a maze easy, and why the cut never calls one maze the hardest.
