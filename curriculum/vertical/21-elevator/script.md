# Script

Status: blocked

No voiceover and no music. The narration is burned into the frame. Twenty-three
words across 14 seconds. The two rules are the lane labels, so the narration
follows you instead of explaining them.

| Frames  | Line                                                     |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 20-99   | You're on 6, going down.                                 |
| 105-166 | Right stops for you.                                     |
| 174-276 | Left makes you wait your turn.                           |
| 284-414 | **Right dropped off all 7 before left dropped you off.** |

Line one comes up on the frame you press the button. Line two is up while the
right car stops at 6 and the left one goes straight past. Line three runs
while the left car serves everyone who pressed before you. The payoff is only
what the frame can prove at that moment: the right building is empty, and the
left one is just letting you out.
