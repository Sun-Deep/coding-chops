# Sources

- Sugiyama, Y. et al. (2008). Traffic jams without bottlenecks: experimental
  evidence for the physical mechanism of the formation of a jam. New Journal
  of Physics 10, 033001.
  https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1367-2630/10/3/033001
  Checked 2026-10-02 for the 22 vehicles, the 230 m circuit, and a jam forming
  with no bottleneck.
- Stern, R. E. et al. (2018). Dissipation of stop-and-go waves via control of
  autonomous vehicles: field experiments. Transportation Research Part C 89,
  205 to 221. https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.01693
  Checked 2026-10-02 for more than 20 vehicles on a ring and waves damped by
  controlling one vehicle's speed. The reel's smoothing rule is a
  simplification, not their controller.
- Treiber, M., Hennecke, A. and Helbing, D. (2000). Congested traffic states in
  empirical observations and microscopic simulations. Physical Review E 62, 1805. The Intelligent Driver Model.
- The run: `scripts/measure-phantom-jam.mjs`, which imports
  `src/vertical/23-phantom-jam/physics.ts`, the same module the reel draws.
