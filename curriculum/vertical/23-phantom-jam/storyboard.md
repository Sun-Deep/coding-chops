# Storyboard

One ring, twice. The elevator and checkout cuts put two sides next to each
other; here two rings side by side made each car about 20 px, so the runs play
one after the other on one big ring, and the first run's result is pinned
under the second's live readout.

## The clock

Simulated seconds 6 onward at 0.4 a frame, twelve times real time, for each
run. The tap is at 10 to 13 s, frames 10 to 17 of each run.

## The shot map

```text
0-18     close on car 0, brake lights come on at frame 10
18-58    camera pulls back to the whole ring; readout fades in at the end
58-190   red travels backwards; average falls to 13 to 14 and stays
190-208  replay: close on car 0 again, the same tap
208-248  pull back; the orange car is visible by its colour and halo
248-420  average recovers above 20; HUMANS ALONE 14 pinned; payoff at 282
```

## Colour

The brake red is a second colour beside the accent. It is the loss colour from
the shared theme and it means braking and nothing else, the one meaning every
driver already reads. Bodies are neutrals; the orange car is the only accent
inside the ring.

## Cue map

```text
the tap          brake, loud, pitched down
a car braking    brake, louder the harder it is, at most every 3 frames
replay starts    return
payoff           settle
```

The sound is the jam: the human run brakes all the way through; the replay
brakes for the first wave and then goes quiet. The last 3.3 s are silent
because nothing on the replay ring brakes, which is the result.

## Checks run

start and the last 3.3 s, explained above. Safe area overlay clean; the ring
stays inside x 930 below y 1000. Cover checked at 1:1 and 3:4.
