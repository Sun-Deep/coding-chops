# Sources

- The results of the Holborn standing-only escalator trial, Londonist:
  https://londonist.com/london/transport/the-results-of-the-holborn-standing-escalator-trial-are-in
- Time Out London on the trial results:
  https://www.timeout.com/london/blog/the-results-of-holborn-stations-standing-only-escalator-trial-are-in-030717
  Checked 2026-10-04: 151 people a minute standing only against 115 with a
  walking lane, about 3,500 more an hour, and Canary Wharf's shorter
  escalators carrying 10% less with standing only.
- Escalator etiquette, Wikipedia:
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escalator_etiquette
  Checked 2026-10-04: 141 against 115 a minute at Holborn in April to May
  2016, about 30% more; 44% walking on the tallest escalators and 53 to 61%
  on the shortest (2002 study); benefit only above about 18.5 m.
- Ianvisits on the trial: https://www.ianvisits.co.uk/articles/holborn-tests-standing-on-both-sides-of-the-escalator-15833/
- The run: `scripts/measure-escalator.mjs`, which imports
