# Storyboard

One shot. The camera stands on the top landing of a Tube escalator shaft and
looks down it, the angle of every photo of a London escalator. Two escalators
side by side under one tunnel: the left one stand right, walk left; the right
one standing on both sides.

## The clock

Simulated second 72 onward, both escalators already full, at twice real time:
slow enough to see walkers climb past the moving steps.

## Drawing

Everything is in metres and projected from one camera, so people near the
camera are about 150 px and people at the bottom a few. The steps move up at
belt speed; the walkers climb on top, legs moving, and the gaps in their lane
are the point. The rider's left is the screen's right, so the walking lane is
the screen-right lane of the left escalator.

## Colour

Neutral coats, a range of skin tones, warm lamp light. Orange means walker:
walkers wear it, and the WALK marking on the landing is in it. The first cut
drew walkers like everyone else, and the creator could not tell which lane
was which or that anyone was walking; orange coats, a bigger stride and the
lane markings fixed that.

## Cue map

```text
someone steps off at the top   tick, lower on the left, higher on the right
payoff                         settle
```

The right escalator's ticks come faster: the result, heard.
