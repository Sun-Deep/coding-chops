# Storyboard

One shot, two kitchens on one clock: first come, first served on top,
shortest first below. The same eight people stand in the same order in both.

## The clock

Forty times real time for the first three seconds, so the quick ones walking
past the orange 3:00 lunch below can be followed; then sixty-six times, so
both kitchens finish together just before the end. Walks take ten frames on
screen whatever the clock is doing.

## Drawing

Eight different people front on, shaded from the left, in shirts,
sweaters, blazers and hoodies, most with an office lanyard, at different
heights and builds. What each one holds tells you their time before the tag
does: a mug for 0:20 and 0:30, a bowl for 0:45 and 1:00, a leftovers box up
to 2:00, a frozen meal carton for 3:00 and up. The first build used one
figure eight times holding the same box; the creator found it flat against
the escalator shaft, so the room got cabinets, tiles, a lamp on the wall and
a floor with depth. Whoever goes next walks to the microwave in front of
the line, so a skip passes in front of the people it skips. Tags alternate
height on stems; at one height they overlapped and nobody's time could be
read.

## Colour

Orange is one person, the 3:00 lunch: coat, lid and tag. Everything else is
neutral; the microwave window warms while food turns.

## The word on the picture

At the payoff the bottom label becomes "Shortest job first" in large type,
so the coding name is on the picture, not only in the subtitle. Gridlock's
lesson: most viewers do not read the subtitle.

## Cue map

```text
someone sets the timer   beep
a dish turning           low hum, for exactly as long as it turns
food done                ding, lower on top, higher below
payoff                   settle
```

## Checks run

Narration lines one and two say both rules in the first six seconds, the
elevator reel's lesson.

Duration 14.06 s. Peak -6.3 dBFS. No silence of 0.35 s or longer. No run of
held frames; scattered single low-motion frames while the last long dishes
turn. Cover checked at 1:1 and 3:4.
