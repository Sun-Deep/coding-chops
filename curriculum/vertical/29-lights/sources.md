# Sources

- Knuth, D. E. The Art of Computer Programming, Volume 3: Sorting and
  Searching, section 6.2.1, binary search: at most floor(log2 n) + 1
  comparisons, 7 for 100 items.
- git-bisect documentation: https://git-scm.com/docs/git-bisect. Binary
  search over a project's history to find the commit that introduced a bug.
- How a series light string fails and is traced with a non-contact voltage
  tester: power is present up to the open bulb and absent after it, so the
  tester finds the break.
- The run: `scripts/measure-lights.mjs`, which imports
  `src/vertical/29-lights/simulation.ts`, the same module the reel draws.
