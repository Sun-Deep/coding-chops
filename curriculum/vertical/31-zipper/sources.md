# Sources

- Minnesota Department of Transportation, zipper merge guidance: use both
  lanes until the merge point, then take turns. The reel's right road sign
  paraphrases its wording.
- Highway Capacity Manual, work zone capacity: one open lane past a lane
  closure carries roughly 1,400 to 1,800 vehicles an hour. The model uses
  1,600 (one every 2.25 s) and sweeps 2.0 and 2.6 s.
- Round-robin scheduling: Silberschatz, Galvin and Gagne, Operating System
  Concepts, CPU scheduling chapter.
- The run: `scripts/measure-zipper.mjs`, which imports
  `src/vertical/31-zipper/simulation.ts`, the same module the reel draws.
