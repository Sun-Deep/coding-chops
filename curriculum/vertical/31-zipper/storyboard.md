# Storyboard

One shot: the same road twice, side by side, at night, each camera riding
above and behind the followed car. At three times real time the car passes
the sign; on the left it moves over and brakes at the back of a long queue,
and the first driver who stays in the ending lane goes past it in the empty
lane. On the right it keeps its lane and reaches a shorter queue 60 m
further on. The crawl runs at fourteen times real time while four more cars
go past on the left. The clock slows to five times as the right road's car
reaches the cones, so turn about there can be seen, then speeds up to bring
the left road's car through. The camera holds on the merge for the payoff
at four times real time.

## How it got here

The race had to stay open until the payoff, the hallway reel's lesson: its
pair finished at second 4 and the rest showed nothing. Here the followed
cars reach the cones at frames 270 and 311, and the "got past you" count
keeps rising until frame 248.

A top-down map was ruled out by the gridlock reel's lesson: a 250 m queue
at phone size makes every car a speck. The camera behind the followed car
keeps it at about 90 px wide and shows what a driver sees: the empty lane
and the cars going past in it.

Cars right behind the camera were drawn as large blocks at the bottom of
the frame, and a fade-in made them look like ghosts. They now enter from
below the frame edge. The camera first followed the car through the cones
and then looked away from the queue; it now stops 12 m before the cones, so
the turn about stays in view for the payoff.

## Colour

Orange is one thing: you. Car bodies are neutral, cones and the sign are
off-white, tail lights are red because tail lights are.
