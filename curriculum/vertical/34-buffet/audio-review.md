VR34 originally sounded its caption beats and left the serving action quiet.
VR24, VR26 and VR33 sound individual arrivals; VR28 sounds each microwave
cycle; VR30 sounds steps and bumps; VR31, VR32 and VR33 keep a quiet physical
room texture under the discrete events. The revision follows those patterns.

## Revised cues

113 ceramic contacts as portions land, 9 serving-tong clicks, 5 visible
footsteps, one warmer ventilation layer, one pass, two dessert-plate success
cues, two blocking or fairness knocks, the average landing and the computing
name. This totals 135. Per-room throttling keeps the accelerated crowd from
becoming an uninterrupted rattle. Diners vary the material pitch slightly;
the upper room is lower and the lower room higher.

Four new WAV sources are generated by scripts/build-sfx.sh, with no third-party
recordings. Source peaks are -25.16 for ventilation, -26.61 for ceramic,
-28.86 for tongs and -28.20 for steps. Gains live in cues.ts. These sources
are 48 kHz mono. The shared README records their source and purpose.

## Verification

The final AAC mix peaks at -5.1 dBFS, with no detected silence of 0.35 seconds
or longer. Mean level is -26.3 dBFS, compared with -25.7 for the kiosk reel;
this is a comparison of whole files, not a loudness target. The material
sounds carry activity while ventilation stays below them. No music or speech.

All 420 decoded video-frame hashes match the previous render. The duration
remains 14 seconds of video, with the same AAC padding as before. The full
revised render played to completion with sound at a 360-pixel display width.
Typecheck, lint, formatting and curriculum validation pass. Cue IDs, frame
ranges and every referenced source file were checked.
