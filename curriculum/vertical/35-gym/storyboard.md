# Storyboard

One shot: the same gym twice, one above the other, seen from the side, at
18 times real time throughout. At frame 0 you walk in at both doors, in
orange. Above, one lifter has the bench: they press, sit up, and sit on it
with a phone for six seconds while you and another lifter stand and wait.
Below, two lifters take turns: one presses while the other rests beside
the bench, then the next steps up, changes the plate, lies back and
presses. At frame 205 the bench comes free below and it is your turn: you
change the plate and press from frame 247. Above, you are still standing
where you stopped. At frame 276 the readouts give your two waits, 11:42
above and 2:03 below, and the lower label becomes "Multitasking".

## Built to read without the narration

The cause is a picture: a person sitting on the only bench with a phone
while people stand around it with arms crossed. Below, the bench is never
idle for long, and the plate changes, the cost, are drawn and tagged.
Every action is on screen for at least a second: a set is about two
seconds, a rest about six, a plate change one to two.
