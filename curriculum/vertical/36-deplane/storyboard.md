# Storyboard

One shot per cabin, both moving together. Frame 0: a wide shot of the plane
at the gate at dusk, the terminal lit behind it. The camera pushes in while
the roof lifts away, and by frame 50 the whole cabin is in view, everyone
seated. At frame 50 the seatbelt sign goes off: above, all 72 stand up,
a beat apart, and the aisle fills; below, nobody moves. The door opens at
frame 150. Above, people walk out row by row; below, row 1 stands, then row
2 once row 1 has gone, and so on. You get off at frame 300 above and 400
below, and the readouts give both times.

## Built to read without the narration

Standing against sitting is the picture: a cabin full of people on their
feet against a cabin of people in their seats. The clocks say how long you
have been on your feet, and the payoff readouts give when you got off.
