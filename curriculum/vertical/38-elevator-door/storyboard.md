# Storyboard

One shot per lobby, both moving together, the camera easing in. Frame 0:
on top a full car has had its doors held for a minute, someone's arm
across the doorway, and the last runner is coming; below the doors are
shutting on a car that is leaving. You walk in from the street. At frame
43 the doors on top shut in your face. In both lobbies you wait while the
floor displays climb and fall. Below, the car comes back at frame 111 and
you ride up at frame 133. On top it comes back late, at frame 151, you get
in first, and the doors are held twice more, until frame 256. You step out
at floor 9 at frame 300 below and 400 on top.

## Built to read without the narration

Held against shut is the picture: an open car with an arm across the
doorway and a counter running, against shut doors and a floor display
moving. Your clock says how long your trip has taken, and the two finals
say who got there first.
