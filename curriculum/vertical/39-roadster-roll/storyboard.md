# Storyboard

One shot per panel, both moving together, the camera following the car
down. Frame 0: the car hangs upside down from the underside of the ramp,
stopped, and starts to drive toward the end. At frame 72 both let go and
the picture slows to about a sixth of real time. On top the car falls flat
and its roof hits the pad at frame 232; it rocks, crushed, in a ring of
dust. Below, a pair of jets fires and the car rolls, the opposite pair
stops it the right way up by frame 178, and four jets underneath brake it
until its wheels touch at frame 282. The car on its roof slides to a stop at frame 319, the one on its wheels
at 362.

## Built to read without the narration

Upside down against the right way up is the picture. The instruments say
why: 1 g on the ramp, 0 g in the fall for both, then 26 g as one roof
crushes against 2 g as the other car's jets brake it.
