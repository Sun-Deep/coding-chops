# Storyboard

One shot per street, both in real time on one clock. Frame 0: under the
awning, the walker stepping out, the runner already running. Your front
leaves the awning on frame 8. The runner is under the shelter at frame 94
with 2,590 drops on; the counter's front bar has shot up and the top bar
barely moved. Above, the walker is still out: the front bar climbs
slowly, the orange top bar climbs at the same rate it did for the runner
and keeps going. At frame 222 the walker's total passes the
runner's. At frame 313 the walker is under cover: the two front bars are
level at 2,172 and the walker's top bar is 3.6 times the runner's. Both
turn to face the street; the cameras push in slowly.

## Built to read without the narration

Two bars per person on one scale. The chalk bars finish level, so the
difference is all orange, the rain from above, and the walker's orange bar
grows for as long as the clock above it runs.
