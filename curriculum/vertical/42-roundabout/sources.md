# Sources

- Treiber, Hennecke and Helbing (2000), the Intelligent Driver Model.
- Highway Capacity Manual, 6th edition: single-lane roundabout entry
  capacity 1380 x exp(-0.00102 x circulating), from a critical headway of
  4.99 s and a follow-up headway of 2.61 s; base saturation flow 1,900
  cars an hour a lane at signals.
- Insurance Institute for Highway Safety: measured conversions to
  roundabouts cut vehicle delay by 89% at three sites (New Hampshire, New
  York, Washington; signals and stop signs) and 65% at 11 sites in Kansas;
  a 2005 study of ten signalised junctions in Northern Virginia estimated
  62 to 74%.
- Unbalanced flows as the known weakness of roundabouts: Iowa/Minnesota
  roundabout toolbox simulation, which found roundabouts faster with
  balanced and unbalanced flows alike.
- The run: `scripts/measure-roundabout.mjs`, which imports
  `src/vertical/42-roundabout/simulation.ts`, the same module the reel
  draws.
