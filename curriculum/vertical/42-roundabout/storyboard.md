# Storyboard

One shot per junction, both on one clock at twice real time. Frame 0: you,
the orange car, drive in from the west in both views. Above, your light
goes red and you stop first at the line while the north-south road runs;
your "stood still" clock climbs to 6.7 s, the relay clicks, the light goes
green and you pull away, leaving the frame at about frame 315. Below, you
slow at the give-way, take a gap without stopping, go round and leave
much earlier; your clock stays at 0.0. The payoff gives the hour's
averages.

## Built to read without the narration

A row of cars stopped at a red stop line above, cars moving the whole time
below, and one orange clock that climbs on top and never moves below.
