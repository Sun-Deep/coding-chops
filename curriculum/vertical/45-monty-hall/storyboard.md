# Storyboard

One shot. Frame 0: three lit doors, 1 2 3, YOU over door 1. The host's
door, 3, swings open on a goat while a clock ticks. Doors 1 and 2 open
together: a goat behind yours, the car behind 2. The camera rises as 299
more games appear around the first, a sea of doors; they open in a wave
out from the first while the tallies count to 10,000. Then the games
slide apart, the ones where sticking won to the left and the ones where
switching won to the right, and the right block is twice as wide.

## Built to read without the narration

Two counters climbing at different speeds, one orange and twice the
other, and two blocks of games, one twice the width of the other.
