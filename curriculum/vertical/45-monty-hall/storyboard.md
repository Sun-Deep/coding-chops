# Storyboard

Two shots. Frame 0: three lit doors, 1 2 3, YOU over door 1. The host's
door, 3, swings open on a goat while a clock ticks. Doors 1 and 2 open
together: a goat behind yours, the car behind 2.

Cut to a game-show studio at eye level. Two contestants stand beside two
empty glass cases on plinths: on the left the one who always sticks, on
the right the one who always switches. One line runs across both cases
at half height, marked 50/50. As the code panel turns into the score and
counts to 10,000, a toy car drops from a chute into a case each time
that choice reaches another 200 wins, landing on the frame the count
passes it. Each case holds 50 cars, all 10,000 games. The switch case
fills past the 50/50 line to 33 cars; the stick case stops at 16, a
third of the way up. The switcher celebrates; the sticker slumps and
stays slumped while the camera eases in.

## Score at full size (24 Oct)

The first cut counted in small corner chips, 18 and 32 px. The ticket reel
posted the day before lost most of its viewers in the first seconds and
showed the code panel and labels too small to read on a phone. The count
is the thing a viewer can check, so it now takes the code panel's place
as the run starts, at 104 px.

## Its own look (24 Oct)

The first cut ended on 300 small glowing doors seen from high up,
sliding into two blocks. The creator pointed out that git bisect, SQL
and PHP2Shell had all ended on the same kind of shot: a field of small
lit objects on a black floor, a high tilted camera, the field splitting
or dimming. The studio replaces it: eye level, people, a set, and a pile
of prizes you can compare with the 50/50 line.

## Built to read without the narration

Two counters climbing at different speeds, one orange and twice the
other, and two glass cases filling with cars, one past the halfway line
and one a third of the way up.
