# Storyboard

Fifteen seconds, the format's ceiling, because the cut has four things to
say: what people saw, how it works, when, and what to do.

One shot. Frame 0: a field of servers from above, the timeline filling in
above it. Four labelled government sites switch off one after another,
then others around them. The camera dives into one server near the front.
An upload request arrives as a stack of parts and PHP unpacks them into
the memory slots, faster and faster, until the limit line turns orange
with the last part only half in. A cleanup bar climbs the rack clearing
each slot and stops under the half-built part; the camera closes on it as
it flares, and orange spreads from it through the machine. The site's
own code, in the cabinet beside it, never lights. The camera pulls back
to the field, the panel turns into three steps, and a patch passes from
the front bringing every site back.

## Built to read without the narration

The timeline is the when, the labels name the sites and the parts, the
orange is the attacker's code, and the three steps are the what to do.
