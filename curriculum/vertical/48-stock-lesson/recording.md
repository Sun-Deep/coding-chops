# Recording

What the creator does, in order.

1. Tick the boxes in `understanding-check.md` you can do out loud without
   the notes. The script stays blocked until every box is ticked.
2. Record each stem in `script.md` as its own file. Six files.
3. Drop them in `audio/master/` in this folder, named `stem-01.wav` to
   `stem-06.wav`. That folder is kept out of Git, like the episode stems.
   A phone's voice memo works; send `.m4a` if that is what it makes and it
   gets converted.
4. Say they are there. The rest is the build: transcription with
   whisper.cpp into `audio/captions/`, the stem lengths into the reel, and
   the animation cut to them.

## How to read it

- Quiet room, phone about a hand's width from your mouth, same place for
  all six so they sound like one take.
- One second of silence before and after each stem. The edges get trimmed.
- Read two or three takes of each and keep the one that sounds like you
  explaining it to a friend, not reading. The script is written to be said.
- Pace about like the stock episode. Faster is fine; slower makes the cut
  longer than a minute.
- Say the numbers as written: "fifteen pairs", "five billion", "two point
  six seconds". The screen shows the measured figure under each one.
- If a line feels wrong in your mouth, change the words in `script.md`
  before recording, not after. Captions come from what you say, so a
  different word on tape is a different word on screen.
