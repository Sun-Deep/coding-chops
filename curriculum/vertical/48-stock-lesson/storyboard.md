# Storyboard

Draft. The shot lengths come from the recorded stems; nothing here is timed
until they exist.

## The look

Eye level, side on, a slow tracking shot like a camera on a dolly beside a
path. Six stone pillars stand in a row on a dark floor, one per day, their
heights 7 1 5 3 6 4, each with its price on its face. A person walks along
in front of them. The cheapest price so far is a lit orange line held at
that pillar's height and carried forward as the walker moves, so the gap
from the line up to today's pillar top is today's profit, measured, not
described.

Nothing here is a grid of glowing objects or a high tilted camera: the
pillars are few and large, the camera is at head height and moves
sideways, and the motion is a person walking.

## Shots, one per stem

1. **The hook.** Close on day one as its pillar rises to 7. At "buy"
   the orange line is drawn at 7, tagged "bought at 7". At "sell" the
   walker steps to day two, which rises only to 1, under the line; a
   dashed drop is tagged "lost 6" and the walker slumps. At "but how" the
   camera pulls back while the other four days rise. At "let's say" the
   line goes and the walker steps back to day one, and as the prices are
   mentioned they light up one after another.

   Rebuilt on 26 Oct after VR50 lost half its viewers between 0:03 and
   0:05 on an opening where only a cursor moved. The first version of this
   reel held its six pillars still for 10 seconds.
2. **The obvious way.** The lowest pillar, 1, and the highest, 7, light;
   an arrow from 1 back to 7 points the wrong way in time and is struck
   out. Then from each pillar an arc to every later one, fifteen arcs in
   order while a counter runs 1 to 15. Then the count jumps to
   4,999,950,000 as the row pulls back into one too long to see the end
   of.
3. **The trick.** Back to six. On day five, the four arcs
   that end there light, then three go dark: only the arc from 1 stays.
4. **The walk.** The walker goes day by day. The orange line starts at 7,
   drops to 1 on day two and stays there. At each later pillar a vertical
   bar rises from the line to the top, labelled with today's profit; the
   best so far is held above. The code panel lights the line doing each
   step.
5. **Time and space.** The nested loop and the one pass side by side in
   the code panel, O(n²) and O(n) at the biggest size in the frame as each
   is said. For space, the walker's two carried values, the orange line
   and the best profit, light as "two numbers" is said, while the row of
   pillars runs on behind them.
6. **What to keep.** Hold on the walker and the line. The conditions on the
   last frame.

## Built to read without the sound

The captions carry every word. The orange line and the profit bars show the
idea with no words at all.
