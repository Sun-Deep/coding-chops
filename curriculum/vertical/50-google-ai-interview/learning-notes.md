# Learning notes

The hook is the creator's: the internet read the news as "LeetCode and DSA
are dead", and the reel says why that is the wrong reading. The new round
grades whether you catch the AI when it is wrong, and catching a wrong
binary search takes knowing how a binary search works. So the thing people
called dead is what the AI round tests.

The code example is LeetCode 704 on purpose: a DSA problem, a one-character
bug (`<` for `<=`), and a plausible fix that moves the bug instead of
removing it. Every result shown is run, on 12,156 cases.

Kept out: anything about whether this is a good way to interview. The reel
reports what changed and what did not.
