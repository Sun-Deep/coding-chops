# Storyboard

One shot of an interview room at eye level, under the code panel. The
headline carries the argument and changes with the voice.

1. "LeetCode and DSA are dead?" over a binary search, LeetCode 704: the kind
   of problem people mean.
2. "Not even close." in orange as the voice says it.
3. The round: the header becomes "Code comprehension round", a bug report
   appears, the question is typed to the AI, and an example fix comes back.
   Chip 1, prompt engineering.
4. The fix lands in the code in orange. The reported case passes; the voice
   says "wrong" and the other case fails in orange, and the laptop's light
   on the candidate turns orange. Chip 2, output validation.
5. "Understand the code": the real fix, `lo <= hi`, and 12,156 of 12,156
   cases pass. Chip 3, debugging.
6. "Only a pilot. LeetCode isn't dead." The pilot's scope and the source
   take the panel's last lines.

## Changes after review (25 Oct)

The creator flagged three things in the first cut. The window with blinds
behind the desk read as a blank screen with no purpose; it is gone. The
two people held one pose for 29 seconds; they now act every beat. The AI
chat was a line at the foot of the code and did not read as an assistant;
it is its own pane under the editor from the first frame, in blue, the
assistant's lane colour.

## Its own look

An office at eye level with two people and a desk, nothing like the
