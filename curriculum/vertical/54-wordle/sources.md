# Sources

- [Pinned word-list directory](https://github.com/3b1b/videos/tree/9add2c2eebcbe40377d8dd08169a42d556dd7ebc/_2022/wordle/data).
  `possible_words.txt` supplies the 2,309 answers. `allowed_words.txt` supplies
  the 12,972 guesses, including answer words. The local fixture retains the
  exact downloaded bytes; the runs record both SHA-256 hashes.
- [3Blue1Brown's Wordle lesson](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/wordle/).
  Feedback partitions and the distinction between a first guess and a solving
  policy. The reel uses expected remaining count rather than entropy.
- [Bhambri, Bhattacharjee and Bertsekas](https://web.mit.edu/dimitrib/www/Wordle_ArXiv_submission.pdf).
  Sequential-search formulation and the distinction between one-step information
  objectives and the expected cost of the whole game.
- [Recorded effects](../../../../public/sfx/kenney/README.md).
  Existing Kenney CC0 recordings. No voiceover or music bed.

The lists describe a historical game configuration. We do not call them the
current NYT answer pool or claim the historical winner is today's optimum.
