# Storyboard

One continuous shot. The hero object is the ride: the two pins and the
streets between them. Camera, scale and motion come from the story, a whole
city seen from above at night, not from an earlier reel.

| Frames     | Picture                                                                                         | Sound                        |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 0 to 2     | Manhattan at an angle, Harlem's pin a third of the way down, Wall Street's below the middle.    |                              |
| 2 to 48    | Both searches leave Harlem. Orange runs down the avenues toward Wall Street; blue stays a pool. | a click, then quick ticks    |
| 48 to 62   | A\* arrives. The route is traced back from Wall Street to Harlem in white.                      | a chime, a switch            |
| 62 to 270  | Blue floods the island and every borough the bike can reach. The camera rises and pulls back.   | slower ticks, growing louder |
| 270        | Dijkstra arrives; the same white route flashes again.                                           | a bell                       |
| 296 to 420 | Verdict over the whole flood: the thin orange thread inside the blue. Camera keeps drifting.    | the verdict landing          |

Text sits in the band above the city (headline, counters) and the band
below it (narration). Only the pin names sit on the scene, beside the pins.

Colour: orange is A\* (the accent, the search the reel is about), blue is
Dijkstra (the lane hue `blueBright`, section 6 of the vertical standard),
chalk is the route both find and the pins. Building windows glow a neutral
cool white, picked at random per building; they carry no meaning and no
number.
