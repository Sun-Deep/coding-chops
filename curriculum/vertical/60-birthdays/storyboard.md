# Storyboard

One continuous shot. The hero object is the class: the 23 and the strings
between them, then the same room as one of a hundred. Camera, scale and
setting come from this story: a classroom from above, then a grid of rooms.

| Frames     | Picture                                                                                        | Sound                       |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 0 to 2     | The room under one light, 23 students in a ring, a date over every head.                       |                             |
| 2 to 110   | Strings appear between pairs, student 1 to everyone first, then faster until all 253 are down. | ticks, thinning into a rush |
| 120        | One string turns orange; its two students raise a hand; both NOV 12 tags turn orange and grow. | a chime                     |
| 182 to 256 | The tags leave; the camera rises; the other 99 rooms come out of the dark, each with its web.  | a rush                      |
| 236 to 304 | Rooms with a shared birthday light orange borders and strings, nearest first, to 53 of 100.    | a click per room            |
| 320 to 420 | Verdict over the grid; camera drifts.                                                          | the verdict landing         |

Colour: orange is a shared birthday and nothing else. Strings and tags are
chalk. Shirts are the muted pack palette.
